### 5. Temple Agamas
Different Agamas in Hindu Sastraas:
- **I. Vaishnavam:**
  - a) Vaikhanasam
  - b) Pancharathram
  - c) Chattada Srivaishnavam
- **II. Saivam:**
  - a) Smardham
  - b) Adi Saiva
  - c) Veera Saiva
  - d) Jangama
  - e) Kapalika, etc.
- **III. Mother Goddesses:**
  - Sakteyam

---

### 8. Philosophies, Cults & Gurus
Different philosophies and cults in Hindu religion, and the gurus who professed them:
- **Alwars** (Vaishnavait Gurus)
- **Nayanars** (Saivait Gurus)
- **Sankaracharya** (Advaitha)
- **Ramanujacharya** (Visishtadvaitha)
- **Madhwacharya** (Dvaithadvaitham)
- **Basava** (Veera Saiva)
